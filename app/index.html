    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play in this order. Story first, missables as they come, then the long collectible and activity grind after the finale. Every stage is built from the official trophy descriptions.

      Missable trophies — do these on time

      These are tied to one-off moments, choices or partner scenes. Keep a manual save before the final mission and before any big partnered scene, and tick each off the moment it pops.
