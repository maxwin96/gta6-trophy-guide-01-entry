Vice City Trophy Atlas — the best offline GTA 6 trophy guide and tracker: all 55 official PlayStation 5 trophies (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden) with guides in our own words and cited sources, a checklist tracker with progress saved in the browser, export and import of progress, filters by kind and grade, a spoiler mode that hides story trophy details until they are earned, and an 8-stage roadmap to the platinum with every missable trophy flagged. Runs fully offline in a desktop browser. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Build the best GTA 6 trophy guide" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory app
```

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a desktop browser, tick off trophies as you earn them, filter by kind or grade, and follow the roadmap to the platinum.
